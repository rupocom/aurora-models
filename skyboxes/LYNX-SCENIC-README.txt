LynX scenic sky pack for Aurora

Extract the models folder beside AuroraSkinchanger.dll (merge it with your existing models folder).
Reload Aurora or use Visuals > World > Import scenic sky files.
Select a LynX preset from Sky & atmosphere.

Alternatively, copy models/materials into CS2 game/csgo, preserving paths.
Original asset paths and simulation data are preserved. Original artwork remains with its creators.
All files match the supplied archive CRC and published SHA-256 manifest. In-game appearance is unverified.
